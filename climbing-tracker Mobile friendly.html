Welcome to ClimbSmart!

Hi there! I'm a passionate climber who developed this tool to optimize my training and prevent injuries. After years of trial and error, I realized that tracking recovery metrics and listening to our bodies is just as important as the training itself.

This tracker uses evidence-based algorithms to analyze your recovery state and provide personalized training recommendations. I hope it helps you on your climbing journey as much as it has helped me!
