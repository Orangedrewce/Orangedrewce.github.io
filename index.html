How to Use ClimbSmart

📊 Daily Entry:

Sleep Quality (1-10): Rate how well you slept. 1 = terrible, 10 = perfect.

Snooze/Wake Time: When you went to bed and woke up. App calculates sleep duration automatically.

Mood State: How you feel mentally. "Psyched" = excited to climb, "Exhausted" = need rest.

Stress Level (1-10): Life stress affecting you today. 1 = zen, 10 = overwhelmed.

DOMS & Tendons: How sore your muscles and tendons feel. Higher = more pain.

Skin Condition: "Perfect" = ready to grip, "Raw/Burning" = needs recovery.

Days Since Last Session: How many days since you last climbed or trained.

Intensity (RPE 1-10, 0=rest): How hard your last workout felt. Skip if it was a rest day.

Completed Today: Check all activities you did today (climbing, weights, cardio, etc).

Pre-Training Meal: Did you eat before training? Affects energy levels.

📈 History & Analytics:

Training History: View all your past entries organized by week. Each entry shows your readiness score, metrics, and recommendations.

Delete Entries: Click the "Delete" button on any log entry to remove it permanently.

Export Data: Export your training data as CSV for analysis in other apps.

Analytics Charts: Toggle different metrics on/off to see trends over time. Click the colored buttons to show/hide specific data lines.

Personalization Factors: See how your sleep patterns and injury history affect your recommendations.

🔄 Data Sync:

Send Data: Generate a 6-digit code to share your training data with another device. The receiving device scans your QR code or enters the code manually.

Receive Data: Enter a 6-digit code from another device, or use the 📷 camera button to scan a QR code and receive training data.

Data Backup: Create backup codes to save your data safely. Copy the generated code and store it somewhere safe.

Restore Data: Paste a backup code to restore your training history from a previous backup.

Data Privacy: All data stays on your device - nothing is sent to external servers unless you manually sync with another device.
