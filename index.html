How to Use ClimbSmart

Sleep Quality (1-10): Rate how well you slept. 1 = terrible, 10 = perfect.

Snooze/Wake Time: When you went to bed and woke up. App calculates sleep duration automatically.

Mood State: How you feel mentally. "Psyched" = excited to climb, "Exhausted" = need rest.

Stress Level (1-10): Life stress affecting you today. 1 = zen, 10 = overwhelmed.

DOMS & Tendons: How sore your muscles and tendons feel. Higher = more pain.

Skin Condition: "Perfect" = ready to grip, "Raw/Burning" = needs recovery.

Days Since Last Session: How many days since you last climbed or trained.

Last Session RPE (1-10): How hard your last workout felt. Skip if it was a rest day.

Completed Today: Check all activities you did today (climbing, weights, cardio, etc).

Pre-Training Meal: Did you eat before training? Affects energy levels.
